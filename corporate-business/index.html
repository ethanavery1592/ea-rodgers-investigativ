Evidence development

Start with the inconsistency. Build toward the explanation.

Corporate matters often involve multiple information sources. The investigative plan may combine surveillance, interviews, records review, public-source research, and timeline development depending on the objective.

01

Inventory & property loss

Identify patterns, access, activity, and other facts connected to unexplained shortages or losses.

02

Records & timeline review

Organize transactions, dates, events, access points, or other records to identify inconsistencies and investigative leads.

03

Interviews & statements

Professional interviews designed to develop first-hand information, identify discrepancies, and clarify events.

04

Lawful surveillance

Field observation and documentation when external activity, movement, or conduct is relevant to the business concern.

Corporate process

Quiet, controlled, and proportional to the problem.

01

Define

Identify the suspected issue, available information, business impact, and decision the investigation needs to support.

02

Preserve

Identify relevant records, timelines, and sources before information disappears or changes.

03

Develop

Conduct interviews, research, fieldwork, or surveillance according to the investigative plan.

04

Report

Present documented findings for management, ownership, or counsel to evaluate and act upon.
