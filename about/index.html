The firm

Built around the facts.

Broadwater Investigative Group provides investigative services to attorneys, businesses, insurance and claims professionals, and private clients throughout the South Carolina Lowcountry.

Our team brings extensive law-enforcement and investigative experience to assignments involving surveillance, interviews and interrogations, witness development, field investigation, research, evidence development, and case documentation.

We approach each matter with a simple objective: determine what needs to be established, use the appropriate investigative methods, and provide clear, well-documented findings.

Broadwater handles matters ranging from sensitive private investigations to litigation support, corporate investigations, and insurance-related assignments.
