Claims investigative services

Field investigation that supports informed claim handling.

01

Workers’ compensation surveillance

Discreet surveillance focused on documented activity, mobility, routines, and observable conduct relevant to the investigative assignment.

02

Activity checks

Targeted field checks designed to verify location, routine, employment-related activity, residence information, or other assignment-specific facts.

03

Witness interviews & statements

Professional interviews with witnesses, employers, neighbors, or other relevant parties when first-hand information may clarify the claim.

04

Scene & fact verification

On-site documentation, photographs, observations, canvassing, and verification of physical circumstances related to an incident or claim.

Claims fact development

The assignment should answer a specific question, not just generate hours.

Effective claims investigation starts by defining what information would materially help the handler or counsel evaluate the matter. The plan can then be tailored to that objective.

01

Surveillance documentation

Chronological notes, video or photography when appropriate, and reporting that distinguishes observation from assumption.

02

Background & public-source research

Lawful research to develop relevant identity, address, business, or publicly available information tied to the assignment.

03

Canvass & witness development

Locating and interviewing people who may have first-hand information about an incident, activity, or relevant circumstance.

04

Potential fraud indicators

Independent fact development when inconsistencies or suspicious circumstances warrant closer review, without substituting investigative observations for the carrier’s ultimate claim determination.
